Airbnb Cleaning Turnover Checklist: The Room-by-Room System

GuestStar Studio · Turnover & Cleaning Systems

A turnover is not "cleaning". It's resetting a small hotel room in a fixed window, to a standard a stranger will judge in a review. The hosts who never get cleanliness complaints don't clean harder — they run a system. Here is the system.

1. Arrival checks (first 5 minutes)

Walk every room before touching anything: damage, left-behind items, anything that smells wrong. Photograph anything unusual and message the host immediately — this protects the cleaner and the deposit claim.

2. Strip and stage

All linens and towels out in one pass, beds stripped to the protector, laundry started before cleaning so it finishes with you. Stage restock items (toilet paper, soap, coffee, filters) at the door so nothing is fetched twice.

3. Room-by-room reset

Bathroom: sanitise every touch surface, mirror streak-free, towels folded the same way every time, amenities refilled to the mark. Kitchen: everything out of the fridge, dishwasher emptied and re-staged, coffee station reset like a product photo. Bedrooms: hotel corners, pillow arrangement identical to the listing photos — guests compare. Living areas: remotes, cushions, throws back to their "listing photo" positions.

4. The photo-finish walkthrough

Stand in each doorway and compare against the listing photos. Anything a guest would notice in the first 10 seconds gets fixed now: crooked frames, a missed hair, a wrinkled duvet. Then lock up, lights as staged, and send the host the "ready" photo set.
