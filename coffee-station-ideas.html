Airbnb Coffee Station Ideas: The Setup Guests Photograph

GuestStar Studio · Guest Experience

Coffee is the most-mentioned amenity in 5-star reviews after cleanliness. Guests don't need a café — they need a station that works at 7am without instructions, and looks good doing it.

The basics that can't fail

One reliable machine (pod machines win on zero learning curve), coffee for the full stay plus one extra morning, tea, sugar, and milk they can find without opening every cupboard. Put the supplies IN the station, not "somewhere in the kitchen".

Layout like a product photo

Tray under everything, machine left, cups visible (not stacked in a cupboard), supplies in two small jars with simple labels, and a tiny framed note: "Help yourself — the beans are from [local roaster], two streets away." That note is the difference between a utility and an experience.

Labels and signs

Labelled jars and a small sign are printable in minutes and make the station read as "designed". Our signage cards ship with the Welcome Book Template Pack.
